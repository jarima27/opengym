# Log like in Hevy. Let the app *pick your next weight*.

Hevy is a great app for logging workouts. Tiza also works out what you should lift on every exercise, tells you why, and with Tiza Pro has an AI Coach that adjusts your plan from your real data.

## Why people switch from Hevy to Tiza

- **Automatic progression**: linear, Greyskull LP, double progression or timed. Tiza raises, repeats or deloads the load from your last session, and tells you why.
- **An AI Coach with nothing to set up**: a plan made for you, a weekly review and a read of every session. Nothing changes without your say-so.
- **Your history comes with you**: import Hevy's CSV, or with Hevy Pro use your API key to bring routines and body weight too.
- **Real privacy**: no ads, and export everything whenever you like.

## Switching from Hevy

### Do I lose my Hevy workouts?
No. Export the CSV from Hevy's settings and upload it when you create your Tiza account. Your weights and records come with you and progression picks up where you left off.

### Can I use Hevy and Tiza side by side while I try it?
Yes. Import again whenever you like: days that already have data are left as they are and only new ones come in.
