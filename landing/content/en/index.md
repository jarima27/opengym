## You know what to lift today. *And why.*

Tiza reads your last session and sets the load for you. Hit every rep and it goes up. Miss and you repeat the weight. Miss three times in a row and it deloads. You always see the rule and the reason, so you can trust the number.

- **Linear**: up 2.5 kg when you complete everything (5 kg for legs, hips and back).
- **Greyskull LP**: two sets and a final set to failure. Beat the target and it goes up; double the reps and it goes up twice as much.
- **Double progression**: reps within a range first, then weight.
- **Timed**: for planks and anything on the clock.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Tiza free?
Yes. Logging, automatic progression, importing your history and stats are free with no time limit. Tiza Pro adds the AI Coach for {{price.monthly}} a month or {{price.annual}} a year, with {{trial_days}} days free.

### What happens when the free trial ends?
If you don't cancel, the plan you chose starts. Cancel before and you're not charged, and you keep using Tiza for free with all your history.

### Can I bring my data from Strong or Hevy?
Yes. Export the CSV from Strong, Hevy or FitNotes and upload it when you create your account. With Hevy Pro you can also import with your API key, routines included.

### What exactly does the AI Coach do?
It proposes a plan from your answers, reviews your week and reads each session with your data. It never changes anything without your say-so, and every change can be undone. Each day's weight is still worked out by the progression engine, which always explains why.

### Is my data mine?
Yes. Export everything whenever you like and delete your account from the app itself. No ads, and we don't sell your data.

### Does it work offline?
Yes. Everything is saved on your phone while you train and syncs when you're back online.

### Which devices does it work on?
In the browser on any phone or computer, and it installs like an app on your home screen. The iPhone and Android apps are on their way.

### How do I cancel?
From Settings → Subscription, in a minute. If you subscribed in the App Store or Google Play, cancel there.

### Why "Tiza"?
It's Spanish for chalk: the gym chalk you put on before the set that matters.
