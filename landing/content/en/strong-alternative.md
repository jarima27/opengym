# Everything you like about Strong, in an app that *knows what to lift*

Strong is fast and simple for logging sets. So is Tiza, and it also works out each exercise's load from your last session and explains why. With Tiza Pro, an AI Coach adjusts your plan from your data.

[Start free](app) [How do I bring my data?](/en/import-from-strong-and-hevy/)
:::

## Why switch from Strong to Tiza

- **Real automatic progression**: hit your reps and it goes up; miss and you repeat; miss three times and it deloads. Linear, Greyskull LP, double progression or timed.
- **Your whole history**: Strong's CSV brings workouts, sets, weights, notes, duration and RPE.
- **An AI Coach**: a plan made for you, a weekly review and a read of every session, in Tiza Pro.
- **No ads**, your data exportable, and the code open source.

### Do my notes and RPE come across?
Yes. Workouts, sets, weights, reps, notes, duration and RPE.

### Can I keep using Strong while I try it?
Yes. Import again whenever you like: days that already have data are left as they are and only new ones come in.
