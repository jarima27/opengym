# Your 5×5, with the weight *always worked out*

Five sets of five, a few exercises and more weight every session. Tiza includes a three-day 5×5 plan ready to go and does the maths for you.

[Start free](app)
:::

## The included 5×5 plan

| Day | Exercises (5×5) |
|---|---|
| A | Barbell squat · Bench press · Barbell row |
| B | Romanian deadlift · Dumbbell overhead press · Lat pulldown |
| C | Leg press · Incline barbell press · Seated cable row |

Swap any exercise, or build your own classic A/B with squat, bench press, row, overhead press and deadlift.

## How it progresses

- **You complete the 5×5**: the weight goes up next session, 2.5 kg (5 kg for legs, hips and back).
- **You miss a rep**: you repeat the weight.
- **You miss three times in a row**: it deloads to 90 % and you build back up.

Everything is configurable per exercise: the increment, the deload and the progression rule.

## 5×5 questions

### Do I need to work out the plates?
No. The plate calculator tells you what goes on each side for your bar and the plates you own.

### Can I move from 5×5 to another program?
Yes. Change the progression rule or the routines whenever you like; your history stays. With Tiza Pro, the AI Coach proposes the next step when you stall.

### Is it free?
Yes: the 5×5 plan, logging and automatic progression are free. The AI Coach is in Tiza Pro, with {{trial_days}} days free.
