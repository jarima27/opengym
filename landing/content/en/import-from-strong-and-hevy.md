# Bring your Strong or Hevy history *in a minute*

Switching apps doesn't mean starting over. Tiza reads what Strong, Hevy and FitNotes export, and progression picks up exactly where you left off.

[Start and import](app)
:::

::: steps
## From Strong

### Export your data
In Strong, open the settings and look for the option to export your data. You get a CSV file with all your workouts.

### Create your Tiza account
Right after you sign up, Tiza asks whether you're coming from Strong or Hevy.

### Pick the file
Tap "Import from Strong" and choose the CSV. You see how many workouts come in before you confirm.
:::

::: steps
## From Hevy

### Export your workouts
In Hevy's settings, look for "Export & Import Data" and export your workouts as CSV.

### Create your Tiza account
The second screen after signing up is the import screen.

### Pick the file… or use your API key
Tap "Import from Hevy" and choose the CSV. With Hevy Pro you can also import with your API key, which brings your routines and body weight too. The key is only used for that import and is never stored.
:::

## What comes across

| | Strong (CSV) | Hevy (CSV) | Hevy Pro (API) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Workouts, sets, weights and reps | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Duration, notes and effort (RPE) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Supersets | No | Yes | Yes |
| Routines | No | No | Yes |
| Body weight | No | No | Yes |

Exercises are matched to Tiza's library (1,324 exercises), and any that aren't there are added as your own: nothing is lost. If the file is in pounds and you use kilos, it's converted. Before you confirm you see how many workouts, sets and exercises come in.

## What if I already imported it?

Tiza doesn't duplicate: days that already have data are left as they are and only new ones come in. It also works with FitNotes and with a spreadsheet that has a date, an exercise and a weight or reps.

::: faq
## Import questions

### Do I lose anything from my history?
Workouts, sets, weights, reps, notes and RPE come across as they are, and exercises that aren't in the library are created as yours. With the Hevy Pro API, routines and body weight too.

### Do I have to stop using Strong or Hevy?
No. Import today, try Tiza for a few weeks and import again later: only what's new comes in.

### How much does importing cost?
Nothing. Importing, logging and automatic progression are free. The AI Coach comes with Tiza Pro, with {{trial_days}} days free.
:::
